# Night Shift: The Okayest Gift

30 seconds, four scenes. Reuses Milo, Dot, the lobby and the parcel. New shared assets added for this episode: desk, counter-room, counter-front, mug, four sound effects, a music bed and five dialogue lines.

1. Closing time (7s, lobby wide). Milo is alone behind the night desk. The door chimes. Dot walks in carrying the tiny parcel. Milo, tired: "Sorry, we're closed. Oh. It's you." He brightens.
2. Delivery (8s, lobby). Dot walks up to the desk: "Special delivery. Extremely special." She sets the parcel on the desk with a thud. The camera pushes in on the desk. Milo is delighted.
3. Unboxing (8s, counter close-up). The parcel wiggles with a rustle. Pop: a mug appears. Milo, deadpan: "It's a mug." Dot: "Read the side."
4. Okayest (7s, counter close-up). The mug turns to show WORLD'S OKAYEST CLERK. Milo is surprised, then beams and waves: "World's okayest clerk. I love it." Door chime sting to end.

Voices: Milo uses Microsoft George, Dot uses Microsoft Zira Desktop (both offline Windows voices). A quiet night pad plays under each scene.
