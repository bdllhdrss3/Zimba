# Special Delivery

Milo is working the night shift in a very quiet parcel office. Dot arrives carrying an absurdly small parcel. Milo approaches, waves, and leans down to inspect it. Dot sets the parcel down with enormous ceremony. They stare at the tiny parcel, then at one another. Milo is delighted. Hold the final shared smile.

This is a reusable, silent 12-second starter. Add prepared dialogue through actor.say when a voice asset is available. Do not regenerate the cast or lobby for the next episode.
